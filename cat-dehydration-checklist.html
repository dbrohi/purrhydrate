How to check the gums and skin safely

Only try these checks if your cat is calm. Gently lift the lip to look at the gums: they should be pink and wet. For the skin, lift a small fold between the shoulder blades and let go; it should settle back quickly. Cornell Feline Health Center notes that older cats may show slow skin even when they are well hydrated, so a slow result is not proof of dehydration, and a normal result does not rule it out. The chart shows the step-by-step checks.

What this checklist cannot do

It cannot tell you why your cat is unwell or how much fluid it has lost. Dehydration is often a sign of another problem, such as vomiting, diarrhea or a long-term illness, so a vet needs to look for the cause. If you are worried at any point, call your vet, even if few boxes are ticked.

What to do next

If your cat is bright, eating and has only mild signs, you can try safe ways to encourage drinking and keep watching. To see how much your cat usually needs, use the calculator.
