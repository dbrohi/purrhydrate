Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Current status

PurrHydrate does not currently use affiliate links, and it does not earn a commission from any product, brand or retailer. Nothing on this Site is paid placement.

If this changes in the future

PurrHydrate may join affiliate advertising programs later, such as Amazon Associates, which let a site earn a commission when a visitor buys through a link. If that happens, we will update this page and our Privacy Policy before any affiliate link goes live, name the program, and place a clear disclosure next to the links.

An affiliate link never increases the price you pay. Retailers set their prices independently of whether you arrive through such a link.

How we write about products

Any product information on this Site is general guidance, such as the kinds of items that may help a cat drink more. It is not a paid endorsement by any brand or manufacturer. If affiliate links are added in the future, our advice will not be changed because of a commission.

Why we're telling you this

The U.S. Federal Trade Commission (FTC) requires that any material financial connection between a publisher and the products it recommends be clearly disclosed. This page exists to be upfront about how PurrHydrate is funded, now and in the future.

Questions

If you have questions about this disclosure, contact us at contact.purrhydrate@gmail.com or through our contact page.
